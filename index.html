Tobias Fellner

PhD student | Quantum Machine Learning | Quantum Reservoir Computing

Hi, I'm Tobias, a PhD student in physics at the University of Stuttgart. I explore how quantum physical systems can be utilized for machine learning, specifically for predicting and analyzing time series data. While my primary focus is on quantum reservoir computing, my research also covers variational quantum algorithms, the robustness of quantum machine learning models, and physical reservoir computing. I work at the Institute for Computational Physics under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Christian Holm, and am affiliated with the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems (IMPRS-IS) and the SimTech Graduate School.
